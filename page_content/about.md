## Company Story
**Title:** From fiscal devices to fully virtual compliance.
**Content:**
FRAME (Fiscal Revenue Automated Management E-Solution) is a leading provider of comprehensive fiscalisation and Fiscal Data Management (FDM) compliance solutions in Zimbabwe. We specialise in virtual fiscalisation, helping businesses streamline financial processes, reduce operational costs, and enhance efficiency while staying fully compliant with local tax regulations.

In 2015, Scentpro Tech signed up as Regional Implementation and Support Partner for Fiscal Revenue Solutions (FRS). FRAME was developed six years later in direct response to evolving ZIMRA requirements. Our technical team brings years of experience in fiscal device installations, support, maintenance, and compliance projects across sectors.

### What we focus on
- Virtual fiscalisation for merchants of all sizes.
- Integration with POS, ERP, and custom systems.
- Reliable, local support for finance and IT teams.
