## Latest Policy Updates
ZIMRA's latest requirements focus on transparency and reducing tax evasion through better data capture and transmission.

## Key Changes
- **Mandatory Fiscalisation:** Approved devices or virtual solutions are mandatory for all registered businesses.
- **Real-Time Transmission:** Data must be sent to ZIMRA in real-time or near real-time.
- **Stricter Penalties:** Increased fines for non-compliance.
- **Expanded Scope:** More sectors are now included in the mandate.

## Business Impact
- **Small Businesses:** May struggle with resources but benefit from operational modernisation and visibility.
- **Large Enterprises:** Need consistent compliance across multiple branches.
- **Pro Tip:** Use cloud-based solutions for scalability and unified reporting.

## Steps to Ensure Compliance
1. Conduct a compliance audit of existing systems.
2. Select an approved solution matching your needs.
3. Train staff on new requirements and systems.
4. Implement changes with a clear rollout plan.
5. Regularly review and maintain the setup.

## Why Early Action Matters
Avoid significant penalties (fines or closure) and allow the team time to adapt to new workflows.
