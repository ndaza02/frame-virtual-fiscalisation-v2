## Anniversary Highlights
FRAME Virtual Fiscalisation marks its first anniversary, celebrating a year of transforming Zimbabwe's digital fiscalisation landscape since its launch in Bulawayo.

## Transforming Fiscalisation
- **Mission:** Provide efficient, transparent, and compliant fiscalisation.
- **Software-Based:** Entirely virtual solution (desktop and mobile) requiring no physical devices or manual paperwork.
- **Efficiency:** Remote installations allow businesses to be up and running within an hour.

## Vision and Impact
- **Trusted Partner:** Aiming to be a key partner for businesses and ZIMRA.
- **Cost Reduction:** Lowers operational costs compared to traditional fiscal devices.
- **Key Benefits:** Easy to use, modern cloud-based, highly efficient, affordable, and minimal support required.

## Regulatory & Local Endorsement
- **ZIMRA:** Commended FRAME for enhancing compliance. Noted that all businesses (including non-VAT registered) will soon be required to fiscalise.
- **Bulawayo City Council:** Deputy Mayor Councillor Godwin Glo recognised FRAME's role in local economic growth and job creation.

## Looking Ahead
FRAME remains dedicated to driving digital transformation and promoting fiscal transparency across Zimbabwe with new features planned for the second year.
