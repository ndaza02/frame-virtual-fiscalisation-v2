## 1. Understand Your Tax Obligations
Key taxes for Zimbabwean businesses:
- **Income Tax:** On business profits.
- **VAT:** Currently 15.5% for registered businesses.
- **PAYE:** For employee salaries.
- **Withholding Taxes:** On certain supplier payments.
- **Customs Duty:** For imports.

## 2. Keep Accurate, Audit-Ready Records
- underpin all tax compliance.
- Use accounting software to automate tracking and reduce errors.

## 3. Stay Updated on Tax Law Changes
- Review annual budget announcements.
- Monitor ZIMRA circulars and official updates.
- Working with a tax professional is recommended.

## 4. Implement a Fiscalisation Solution
- Use ZIMRA-approved tools (devices or virtual solutions).
- Keep software updated and maintain backups.
- Train staff on capturing every sale correctly.

## 5. Plan Ahead for Tax Payments
- Regularly set aside funds for taxes.
- Make quarterly estimated payments if required.
- Build tax planning into business routines to avoid cash flow shocks.
