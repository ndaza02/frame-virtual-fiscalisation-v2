## Workflow Overview
**Title:** A simple, four-step workflow.
**Subtitle:** Designed to plug into existing systems while meeting every ZIMRA requirement.

1. **Connect your POS, ERP, or invoicing tool:** Integrate via API or apps. Implementation guidance provided.
2. **FRAME validates and fiscalises each invoice:** Applies ZIMRA rules and signs data. Catches missing details early.
3. **Secure transmission to ZIMRA FDMS:** Secure, encrypted transmission. Queues and syncs when connectivity drops.
4. **Monitor, reconcile, and report:** Use dashboards and exports to track fiscal receipts and reconcile systems.
