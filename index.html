About FRAME

Leading the digital transformation of fiscal compliance in Zimbabwe

FRAME (Fiscal Revenue Automated Management E-Solution) is a leading provider of comprehensive fiscalisation and Fiscal Data Management (FDM) compliance solutions in Zimbabwe. Specializing in virtual fiscalisation, helping businesses streamline financial processes, reduce operational costs, and enhance efficiency while ensuring full compliance with local tax regulations.

Developed in response to ZIMRA requirements, backed by a technical team with extensive experience in fiscal device installations, support, maintenance, and compliance. Our journey began in 2015 when we signed up as Regional Implementation and Support Partners for Fiscal Revenue Solutions (FRS).
