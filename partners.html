Partner FAQs

How much can I earn?

We offer competitive commissions based on successful sign-ups and the depth of the partner relationship. Detailed commission structures are shared during onboarding.

Is there a cost to join?

No. Joining the FRAME Partner Program is completely free. We invest in partners who are committed to delivering precision fiscalisation solutions.

Who should I refer?

Any Zimbabwean business that needs to maintain ZIMRA compliance through fiscal records, from small merchants to multi-branch networks.

What kind of support will I get?

Partners get direct access to our technical leads for integration help and commercial support for closing deals and tenders.
