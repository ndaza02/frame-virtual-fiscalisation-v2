A simple, four-step workflow.

Designed to plug into existing systems while meeting every ZIMRA requirement.

1

Connect

Connect your POS, ERP, or invoicing tool. Integrate via API or apps. Implementation guidance provided.

2

Fiscalise

FRAME validates and fiscalises each invoice, applying ZIMRA rules and signing data. Catches missing details early.

3

Transmit

Secure, encrypted transmission to ZIMRA FDMS. Queues and syncs automatically when connectivity drops.

4

Monitor

Use dashboards and exports to track fiscal receipts, reconcile systems, and generate reports.
