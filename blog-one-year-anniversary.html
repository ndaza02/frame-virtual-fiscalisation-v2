Celebrating One Year of Growth

FRAME marks its first anniversary of transforming Zimbabwe's digital fiscalisation landscape since its launch in Bulawayo.

FRAME Virtual Fiscalisation marks its first anniversary, celebrating a year of transforming Zimbabwe's digital fiscalisation landscape since its launch in Bulawayo. This milestone represents a significant shift in how local businesses approach compliance and operational efficiency.

Transforming Fiscalisation

Our mission has always been clear: to provide an efficient, transparent, and compliant fiscalisation ecosystem that grows with our clients.

  • Software-Based: Entirely virtual solution (desktop and mobile) requiring no physical devices or manual paperwork.
  • Efficiency: Remote installations allow businesses to be up and running within an hour.

Vision and Impact

"The goal was never just to follow the rules, but to redefine them for a digital-first economy."

As a trusted partner, we've focused on cost reduction and operational ease:

  • Cost Reduction: Lowers operational costs significantly compared to traditional physical fiscal devices.
  • Key Benefits: Modern cloud-based architecture, highly efficient processing, and minimal support requirements.

Regulatory & Local Endorsement

The reception from both regulatory bodies and local government has been overwhelmingly positive. ZIMRA has commended FRAME for enhancing compliance, noting that the mandate for fiscalisation will soon expand to include all businesses, regardless of VAT registration status.

The Bulawayo City Council, represented by Deputy Mayor Councillor Godwin Glo, has also recognised FRAME's role in driving local economic growth and fostering job creation in the tech sector.

Looking Ahead

As we enter our second year, FRAME remains dedicated to driving digital transformation and promoting fiscal transparency across Zimbabwe. We have an exciting roadmap of new features planned that will further simplify business management for our users.
